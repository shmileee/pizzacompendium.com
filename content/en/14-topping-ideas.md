## Topping ideas {#topping-ideas}

The Italian rule is that less is more. A few good ingredients taste better than a mountain of toppings that weighs the dough down and releases water. Here are the classic combinations:

- **Margherita** – tomato sauce, mozzarella fior di latte, fresh basil, extra virgin olive oil.
- **Marinara** – tomato sauce, garlic, oregano and olive oil, with no cheese.
- **Bianca** – a pizza without tomato, e.g. mozzarella with olive oil and herbs (in Rome the name also covers a plain flatbread with only olive oil and salt).
- **Diavola** – tomato sauce, mozzarella and spicy salami.
- **Prosciutto e funghi** – tomato sauce, mozzarella, cooked ham and mushrooms (best lightly sautéed first).
- **Quattro formaggi** – four cheeses, e.g. mozzarella, gorgonzola, Parmesan and fontina.
- **Prosciutto crudo e rucola** – tomato sauce and mozzarella, with raw cured ham (for example Parma ham) and rocket added after baking.

::: tip
It is best to add delicate ingredients such as raw cured ham and rocket only after baking. Fresh basil can go on after baking or, as on a Neapolitan pizza, before baking (best under the cheese, so that it does not burn). Use ingredients rich in water (e.g. fresh tomatoes) sparingly.
:::
